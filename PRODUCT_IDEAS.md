## 🎯 The Core Problem

Traditional meme generators (like Imgflip or basic template apps) force users through a tedious, repetitive workflow:
1. Search through hundreds of static image templates.
2. Manually brainstorm text that fits that specific image layout.
3. If the meme falls flat, start over from scratch.
4. Repeat separately for video, reels, and Twitter copy.

In **2026**, content creation happens across multiple formats and platforms simultaneously. Creators need tools that work at the **speed of thought**.

## 🧠 Core Product Pillars

### 1. One Idea → Many Formats
A single thought (*"When you promise yourself you will go to sleep early but it is 3 AM"*) immediately powers:
- 6 visual memes with distinct comedic tones.
- A vertical short-form video script with sound cues.
- Social media captions tailored for character limits and audience norms.

### 2. Comedic Tone Modifiers
Humor is not one-size-fits-all. MemeForge allows creators to dial between:
- **😊 Relatable**: Broad, relatable, universal situations.
- **🔥 Savage**: Sharp, playful, self-deprecating burns.
- **💀 Unhinged**: Surreal, chaotic, Gen-Z absurdity.
- **🧠 Clever**: Irony, nuance, and smart wordplay.
- **❤️ Wholesome**: Uplifting and empathetic moments.
- **🇮🇳 Desi**: Cultural references, Hinglish slang, and Bollywood/Cricket lore.

### 3. Meme DNA & Creative Intelligence
Every meme is scored for structural comedic strength (contrast between premise and punchline, text economy, shareability) with recommendations to maximize virality.

### 4. 100% Client-Side Speed & Privacy
No mandatory login, no watermarks, no server lag. Everything operates instantly in the browser.
