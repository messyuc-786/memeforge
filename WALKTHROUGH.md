## 1. Landing Page & "Idea In, MEME OUT!" Flow

The homepage is designed around the **2026 AI-first creation model**:
- **Electric Aurora Backdrop**: Dynamic animated aurora mesh with layered lavender, bubblegum pink, cyan, and sunshine gold highlights over a subtle dotted grid pattern.
- **Console Input**:
  - Enter any thought or situational prompt (*"When your manager says the meeting will only take 5 minutes"*).
  - Select from 7 humor tones (*😊 Relatable, 🔥 Savage, 💀 Unhinged, 🧠 Clever, ❤️ Wholesome, 🇮🇳 Desi, 🤪 Absurd*).
  - Click **`✨ FORGE MEME UNIVERSE →`** to trigger the content synthesis pipeline.

---

## 2. Content Multi-Verse Engine

Instead of producing a single meme, the engine generates an entire multi-channel content pack:
- **6 Multi-Tone Static Memes**:
  - `Classic Meme`: Universal punchline and framing.
  - `Relatable Meme`: Everyday situational humor.
  - `Savage Burn`: Edgy, sarcastic, self-deprecating roast.
  - `Desi / Hinglish`: Indian pop-culture references & slang.
  - `Corporate Satire`: Workplace & office life humor.
  - `Absurd / Unhinged`: Surreal Gen-Z punchlines.
- **Short-Form Video Reel Script**:
  - 9:16 hook text overlay + suggested audio track recommendation.
- **Social Multi-Pack**:
  - Formatted X/Twitter viral post, WhatsApp 1-liner, and relevant trending hashtags.

---

## 3. Meme DNA & AI Creative Scoring

Each generated meme contains a **Meme DNA Profile**:
- **Trait Breakdown**:
  - *Emotion*: (e.g. Panic, Satisfaction, Existential Dread)
  - *Tone*: (e.g. Sarcastic, Wholesome, Chaotic)
  - *Audience*: (e.g. Gen-Z, Tech Workers, Cricket Fans)
  - *Energy*: (e.g. High Octane, Low-Key)
  - *Shareability*: (e.g. Group Chats, Twitter Threads)
- **Creative Score (0–100)**: Evaluates punchline contrast, brevity, and format alignment.
- **3 Actionable AI Suggestions**: Specific guidance on improving impact and timing.

## 4. 10-Way Instant Remix Matrix

From any meme card, open the **Remix Drawer** to transform the meme in 1 click:
1. 🔀 **Random Template Swap**: Re-binds the idea to a different iconic format.
2. 🔥 **Make More Savage**: Amplifies the burn level.
3. ❤️ **Make More Wholesome**: Shifts tone to uplifting and heartwarming.
4. 🤪 **Make More Absurd**: Injects surreal Gen-Z chaotic humor.
5. 🧢 **Gen-Z Slang Overhaul**: Replaces text with current internet slang (*No cap, fr fr, bet, cooked*).
6. 🇮🇳 **Desi Mode**: Translates into Hinglish cultural context.
7. ✂️ **Ultra-Short Punchline**: Condenses text to 3–5 high-impact words.
8. 🎥 **Convert to 9:16 Video**: Transfers the hook into the Video Meme Studio.

## 5. 9:16 Video Meme Studio

Designed for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts:
- **Synchronized Text Overlays**: Top and bottom caption banners rendered directly on top of video.
- **Playback Controls**: 0.5x slow-motion, 1x normal, 1.5x speed, 2x fast forward.
- **AI Hook Suggestions**: 1-Click hooks tailored for short-form retention.

## 6. HTML5 Canvas Pro Studio & GPU Shaders

A desktop-grade canvas editor running on client-side HTML5 Canvas:
- **Layer System**: Add, reorder (bring forward/send backward), duplicate, and delete elements.
- **Speech Bubbles**: 6 styles (*Comic dialogue, Thought cloud, Scream burst, Whisper, Modern chat pill*) with draggable tail anchor points.
- **6 GPU Cartoon Shaders**:
  - 🧸 *Cute Cartoon*: Soft contrast with warm bloom.
  - 📰 *Comic Book*: Halftone dot rasterization with inky outlines.
  - 🎬 *Pixar 3D*: Smooth volumetric lighting highlights.
  - ✏️ *Pencil Sketch*: High-contrast graphite cross-hatching.
  - 🤪 *Deep Fried*: Hyper-saturated color crunch and pixel sizzle.
  - 🎌 *Anime Cel-Shade*: Crisp edge detection with cel-shaded colors.
- **Sticker Vault**: Classic memes (*Doge, Pepe, Gigachad, Wojak*), Thug Life accessories, and custom PNG uploads.
- **Export Options**: HD PNG, JPEG, WebP, 1-Click Clipboard Copy with confetti celebration, and native Web Share.

## 10. Local Persistence & Privacy Architecture

- All project state is auto-saved to `localStorage` under `memeforge_autosave_project`.
- 100% Client-Side: Images, canvas pixels, and recordings are never transmitted to any external server.
- Error Boundary protection ensures zero blank screen crashes.
